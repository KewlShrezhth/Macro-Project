Part 1 · Security
01
Color-Shifting Ink
The corner number turns from copper to green when tilted. No home printer or copier can reproduce it.
02
Security Thread
Woven inside the paper, printed “USA 100”, and placed in a different spot on every denomination.
03
Watermark Window
Hold it to the light and a faint portrait appears inside the paper itself. It can’t be printed.
Part 2 · Accessibility
01
Tactile Raised Dots
Count them by touch: one dot on the $1, up to six on the $100.
02
High-Contrast Numerals
Huge, dark numbers on a light panel, in multiple spots on both sides, readable for people with low vision.
