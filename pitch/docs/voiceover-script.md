# Voice-over script: The New American Note Series (2:30)

Timestamps are when each line should start, matched to what's on screen in the film.

**[0:00] HOOK**
Every day, we hand over cash without thinking twice. That only works if money is hard to fake and easy for everyone to use. So we redesigned it. This is the New American Note Series: money you can trust.

**[0:16] SECURITY**
First, security. Every note has three features a counterfeiter can't copy.

**[0:22]** One: a color-shifting numeral. Tilt the bill and the number in the corner turns from copper to green. A photocopy can't do that.

**[0:33]** Two: a security thread woven into the paper. It reads "USA" and the value, and it sits in a different spot on every bill, so nobody can bleach a five and reprint it as a hundred.

**[0:44]** Three: a watermark window. It looks blank, but hold the bill up to the light and a second portrait appears, built into the paper itself.

**[0:55] ACCESSIBILITY**
Next, accessibility. Money should work for everyone, including the millions of Americans who are blind or have low vision.

**[1:00]** Feature one: large, high-contrast numbers. Dark numerals on light panels, big enough to read even when your vision is blurry.

**[1:11]** Feature two: raised tactile dots. One dot on the one, up to six on the hundred, so you can tell bills apart by touch.

**[1:22]** And every value is a different length, seven millimeters longer each step, from 156 millimeters on the one to 191 on the hundred.

**[1:33] THE ECONOMY**
So why does this matter for the economy?

**[1:35]** First, money is a store of value. When it's hard to fake, people trust what they save.

**[1:44]** Second, it's a medium of exchange. Bills that are quick to check make every trade faster and safer.

**[1:52]** That confidence boosts consumer spending, the C in aggregate demand, and businesses can invest without worrying about fake cash.

**[2:00]** And because the world trusts the dollar, it stays the global reserve currency.

**[2:05] COST-BENEFIT**
Is it worth it? There are costs: new printing plates, upgrading ATMs and vending machines, and teaching the public. But those costs are one-time. The benefits last: less counterfeiting, access for blind and low-vision Americans, and more trust in the dollar.

**[2:22] CLOSE**
The New American Note Series. Secure. Accessible. Built for trust.
